Meet your new sidekick

Useful tools. A friendly feel.

Orange Otter is being shaped around the things that make an AI workspace feel simple, capable, and genuinely enjoyable to use.

Chat that flows

Ask questions, explore ideas, and keep each conversation together instead of cluttering your recent chats.

Voice, naturally

Use your microphone to talk, and hear spoken responses with the selected Orange Otter voice.

Recent chats, organized

One entry per conversation, with a helpful title that reflects what you were talking about.

A workspace for ideas

Bring brainstorming, learning, writing, and project planning into one familiar place.

Made to keep improving

New ideas and refinements are part of the journey. The experience will continue to grow as the beta develops.

Desktop-first

A focused desktop experience designed to feel at home on your computer, with a clean interface and room to think.

A more natural conversation

Sometimes, it's easier to just talk.

Start a voice conversation, speak at your own pace, and use the on-screen microphone control to mute or unmute yourself. Orange Otter's voice experience is being refined alongside the rest of the app.
